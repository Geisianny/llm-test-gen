Feature: Bootstrap a Alma 8 Salt minion
Tags: ['@alma8_minion']

Scenario 1: Clean up sumaform leftovers on a Alma 8 Salt minion
  When  I perform a full salt minion cleanup on "alma8_minion"

Scenario 2: Log in as admin user
  Given  I am authorized for the "Admin" section

Scenario 3: Bootstrap a Alma 8 Salt minion
  When  I follow the left menu "Systems > Bootstrapping"
  Then  I should see a "Bootstrap Minions" text
  When  I enter the hostname of "alma8_minion" as "hostname"
  And  I enter "linux" as "password"
  And  I select "1-alma8_minion_key" from "activationKeys"
  And  I select the hostname of "proxy" from "proxies" if present
  And  I click on "Bootstrap"
  And  I wait until I see "Bootstrap process initiated." text
  And  I wait until onboarding is completed for "alma8_minion"

Scenario 4: Check connection from Alma 8 Salt minion to proxy
  Tags: ['@proxy']
  Given  I am on the Systems overview page of this "alma8_minion"
  When  I follow "Details" in the content area
  And  I follow "Connection" in the content area
  Then  I should see "proxy" short hostname

Scenario 5: Check registration on proxy of Alma 8 Salt minion
  Tags: ['@proxy']
  Given  I am on the Systems overview page of this "proxy"
  When  I follow "Details" in the content area
  And  I follow "Proxy" in the content area
  Then  I should see "alma8_minion" hostname

Scenario 6: Check events history for failures on Alma 8 Salt minion
  Given  I am on the Systems overview page of this "alma8_minion"
  Then  I check for failed events on history event page
