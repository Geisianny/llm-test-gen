Feature: Applying promotion with an expiration date
Description: In order to pay proper amount after using the promotion
    As a Visitor
    I want to have promotion's discounts applied to my cart only if the given promotion is valid
Tags: ['@receiving_discount']

Background: 
  Given  the store operates on a single channel in "United States"
  And  the store has a product "PHP T-Shirt" priced at "$100.00"
  And  there is a promotion "Christmas sale"
  And  this promotion gives "$10.00" discount to every order

Scenario 1: Receiving a discount from a promotion which does not expire
  Tags: ['@ui', '@api']
  Given  this promotion expires tomorrow
  When  I add product "PHP T-Shirt" to the cart
  Then  my cart total should be "$90.00"
  And  my discount should be "-$10.00"

Scenario 2: Receiving no discount from a valid but expired promotion
  Tags: ['@ui', '@api']
  Given  this promotion has already expired
  When  I add product "PHP T-Shirt" to the cart
  And  my cart total should be "$100.00"
  And  there should be no discount

Scenario 3: Receiving a discount from a promotion which has already started
  Tags: ['@ui', '@api']
  Given  this promotion has started yesterday
  When  I add product "PHP T-Shirt" to the cart
  Then  my cart total should be "$90.00"
  And  my discount should be "-$10.00"

Scenario 4: Receiving no discount from a promotion that has not been started yet
  Tags: ['@ui', '@api']
  Given  this promotion starts tomorrow
  When  I add product "PHP T-Shirt" to the cart
  And  my cart total should be "$100.00"
  And  there should be no discount
