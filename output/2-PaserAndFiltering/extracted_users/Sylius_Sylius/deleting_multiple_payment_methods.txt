Description: In order to remove test, obsolete or incorrect payment methods in an efficient way

    I want to be able to delete multiple payment methods at once
Tags: ['@managing_payment_methods']

  And  the store has also a payment method "Bank transfer" with a code "transfer"
  And  the store has also a payment method "Cash on Delivery" with a code "cash_on_delivery"
  And  I am logged in as an administrator

Scenario 1: Deleting multiple payment methods at once
  Tags: ['@no-api', '@ui', '@mink:chromedriver']
  When  I browse payment methods
  And  I check the "Offline" payment method
  And  I check also the "Bank transfer" payment method
  And  I delete them
