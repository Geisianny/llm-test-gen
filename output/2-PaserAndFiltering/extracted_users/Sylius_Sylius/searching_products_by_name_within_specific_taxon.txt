Tags: ['@viewing_products']

  And  this product belongs to "Guns"

  Tags: ['@api', '@ui']

  Tags: ['@api', '@ui']
  When  I browse products from taxon "Guns"
  And  I search for products with name "Magnum"

  And  I should see the product "Glock 17"

  Tags: ['@api', '@ui']
