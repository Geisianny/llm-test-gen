Tags: ['@cart_inventory']

  And  "T-Shirt Mononoke" product is tracked by the inventory

  And  I am a logged in customer

Scenario 1: Preventing from adding more items to the cart than it's available in stock
  Tags: ['@api', '@ui', '@javascript']
