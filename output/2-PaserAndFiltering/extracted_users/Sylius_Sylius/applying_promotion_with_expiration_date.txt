Feature: Applying promotion with an expiration date
Description: In order to pay proper amount after using the promotion
    As a Customer
    I want to have promotion's discounts applied to my cart only if the given promotion is valid
Tags: ['@receiving_discount']

Background: 
  Given  the store operates on a single channel in "United States"
  And  the store has a product "PHP T-Shirt" priced at "$100.00"
  And  there is a promotion "Christmas sale"
  And  this promotion gives "$10.00" discount to every order
  And  I am a logged in customer

Scenario 1: Receiving a discount from an ongoing promotion
  Tags: ['@api', '@ui']
  Given  this promotion is valid until tomorrow
  And  I added product "PHP T-Shirt" to the cart
  When  I check the details of my cart
  Then  my cart total should be "$90.00"
  And  my discount should be "-$10.00"

Scenario 2: Receiving no discount from an expired promotion
  Tags: ['@api', '@ui']
  Given  this promotion has already expired
  And  I added product "PHP T-Shirt" to the cart
  When  I check the details of my cart
  Then  my cart total should be "$100.00"
  And  there should be no discount applied

Scenario 3: Receiving a discount from a promotion that has already started
  Tags: ['@api', '@ui']
  Given  this promotion started yesterday
  And  I added product "PHP T-Shirt" to the cart
  When  I check the details of my cart
  Then  my cart total should be "$90.00"
  And  my discount should be "-$10.00"

Scenario 4: Receiving no discount from a promotion that has not started yet
  Tags: ['@api', '@ui']
  Given  this promotion starts tomorrow
  And  I added product "PHP T-Shirt" to the cart
  When  I check the details of my cart
  Then  my cart total should be "$100.00"
  And  there should be no discount applied
