Feature: Onboarding Terms of Use
Tags: ['@androidApp', '@regression', '@onboarding']

Scenario 1: Install and launch app
  Given  the app displayed the splash animation
  And  the Welcome Screen is displayed
  When  I tap "Get started"
  Then  Terms of Use is displayed

Scenario 2: Attempt to bypass ToU without accepting terms
  When  I kill the app
  And  I relaunch the app
  And  the app displayed the splash animation
  Then  the Welcome Screen is displayed
  When  I tap "Get started"
  Then  Terms of Use is displayed

Scenario 3: Agree to terms
  When  I agree to terms
  Then  Terms of Use is not displayed

Scenario 4: Restart app after accepting terms
  When  I kill the app
  And  I relaunch the app
  And  the app displayed the splash animation
  Then  the Welcome Screen is displayed
  When  I tap "Get started"
  Then  Terms of Use is not displayed
