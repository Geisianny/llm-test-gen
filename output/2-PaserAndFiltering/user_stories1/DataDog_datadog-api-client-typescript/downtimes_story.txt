[Downtiming](https://docs.datadoghq.com/monitors/notify/downtimes) gives you greater control over monitor notifications by allowing you to globally exclude scopes from alerting. Downtime settings, which can be scheduled with start and end times, prevent all alerting related to specified Datadog tags.  **Note:** `curl` commands require [url encoding](https://curl.se/docs/url-syntax.html).
