SLO Status Corrections allow you to prevent specific time periods from negatively impacting your SLO’s status and error budget. You can use Status Corrections for various purposes, such as removing planned maintenance windows, non-business hours, or other time periods that do not correspond to genuine issues. See [SLO status corrections](https://docs.da tadoghq.com/service_management/service_level_objectives/#slo-status- corrections) for more information.
