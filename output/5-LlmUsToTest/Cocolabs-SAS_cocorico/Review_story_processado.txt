  Given there are two registered users "asker1" and "offerer1"
  And "asker1" has made a transaction with "offerer1"
  When "asker1" adds a review for "offerer1"
  And rates "offerer1" with 5 stars
  Then the review is visible on "offerer1"'s profile
  And "offerer1"'s rating is updated to reflect the new review

  Given there are two registered users "asker1" and "offerer1"
  And "asker1" has made a transaction with "offerer1"
  And "asker1" has added a review for "offerer1" with 5 stars
  When "asker1" views "offerer1"'s profile
  Then "asker1" sees the review they left for "offerer1"
  And "asker1" sees "offerer1"'s overall rating

Scenario: User tries to add a review for a non-existent user
