The LocalSupport system, used to manage Harrow's community organizations and services, must allow a charity worker to edit the public information of their own organization, such as its website, address, contact details, and categories, controlling which data is displayed to the public. Only associated users may perform these edits, while other users should not see any editing options. The system must also accept protocol-less URLs, handle external failures without preventing updates, and immediately reflect changes on the organization's page.
