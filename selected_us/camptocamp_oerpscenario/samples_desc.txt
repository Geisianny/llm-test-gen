OERPScenario is a functional testing tool for OpenERP/Odoo based on BDD. It ensures the automated validation of critical processes, such as billing and bank reconciliation. The system must allow users to create and update partners, use variables stored across scenarios, apply helpers to locate records, and validate invoices through internal methods. These scenarios ensure that basic operations, such as resource creation, data updates, and document validation, work correctly, maintaining the consistency of the expected behavior in Odoo.
