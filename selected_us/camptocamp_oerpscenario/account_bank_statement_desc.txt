OERPScenario is a functional testing tool for OpenERP/Odoo based on BDD. It ensures the automated validation of critical processes, such as billing and bank reconciliation. The system must allow scenarios to register invoices, create and import bank statements, and confirm reconciliations, verifying residual amounts and status after each operation. If invalid situations exist, such as already reconciled invoices, the system must prevent new reconciliations and issue appropriate alerts.
