OERPScenario is a functional testing tool for OpenERP/Odoo based on BDD. It ensures the automated validation of critical processes, such as billing and bank reconciliation. The system must allow registering and validating invoices, executing partial and full payments in different currencies, and correctly recalculating the residual amount after each operation. It must also ensure that when an invoice is fully settled, its status is updated to "paid," maintaining the consistency of the calculations.
