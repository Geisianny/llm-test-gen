OERPScenario is a functional testing tool for OpenERP/Odoo based on BDD. It ensures the automated validation of critical processes, such as billing and bank reconciliation. The system must allow users to create sales orders using DSL, register order lines, confirm sales, generate associated invoices, and validate the resulting accounting entries. It also verifies the correct handling of exceptions, such as the cancellation and reopening of invoices, ensuring that the sales order maintains the expected state throughout the entire workflow.
