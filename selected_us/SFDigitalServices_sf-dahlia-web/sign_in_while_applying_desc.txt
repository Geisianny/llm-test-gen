DAHLIA is a specialized application module of San Francisco's affordable housing portal. The system must allow users with an account to authenticate themselves while anonymously filling out an application. This includes intelligent reconciliation of conflicting data (such as date of birth in senior-specific listings), options to continue anonymously, create a new account, or use existing account data, as well as management of saved drafts or previously submitted applications. This ensures flexibility, prevents loss of progress, and maintains the integrity of submissions.
