CoreShop is an e-commerce system. The system must work with gross values, taxes, and tax rule groups, ensuring that products added to the cart have their prices correctly adjusted. When creating an active voucher-type rule with a defined code and applying a percentage discount, the cart must automatically recalculate the item value and final total, reflecting the applied percentage and maintaining the consistency of tax-inclusive values. In this case, a 10% discount on a product priced at 100.00 should reduce the total to 90.00, ensuring that the rule and voucher mechanism operates correctly and predictably.
